# Provider logos

Shapes are downloaded from Simple Icons (CC0) and Lobe Icons (MIT), not generated or redrawn. Original SVG files and license notices are retained; PNG files are rasterized copies for offline native display. SOURCES.json records URLs and SHA-256 values. Runtime presentation tints monochrome shapes for light/dark contrast.

Brands: OpenAI, Google Gemini, Meta, xAI (Grok), Qwen, Cloudflare, Apple, NVIDIA (Nemotron/Parakeet) and Bilibili (Index-Translate publisher). Brand names and marks remain the property of their owners; inclusion identifies configured services and does not imply endorsement. This app does not claim the logo artwork as its own.
